# Framework Comparison: v0.1 vs. v0.3

**Context:** This document compares reflection approaches for the same week (Jan 28 - Feb 3, 2026) using two different framework versions. Version 0.1 was the baseline approach. Version 0.3 incorporates research findings on hypothesis disclosure, confirmation bias, and therapeutic best practices.

---

## Key Philosophical Differences

### v0.1 Approach: Interpretation-Forward
- **Default stance:** Disclose patterns, hypotheses, and interpretations directly
- **Questions:** Often investigative, asking about origins and causes
- **Tone:** More analytical and interpretive
- **Hypothesis presentation:** Single interpretations presented as likely
- **User role:** Responds to AI's interpretations

### v0.3 Approach: Evocation-Forward
- **Default stance:** Ask evocative questions, offer hypotheses on-demand only
- **Questions:** Open-ended, invite user's own interpretation
- **Tone:** Tentative, curious, epistemically humble
- **Hypothesis presentation:** Multiple competing alternatives presented equally
- **User role:** Generates own insights with AI support

## Side-by-Side Comparison: Daily Reflections

### Example 1: January 30, 2026 (Core Belief Identification Day)

#### v0.1 Daily Frontend

**Structure:**
- Key Themes section
- Connections to Previous Themes section
- Emotional Tone section
- Writing Style section
- Questions section

**Key Themes excerpt:**
> "You then applied the iceberg model from The Connection Playbook to identify your core belief: 'I am not enough, I am a fraud, I am dumb.'"

**Connections excerpt:**
> "This 'kings on thrones' pattern explains the validation-seeking you named on Jan 28. It's not just about Jovana - it's systemic. You create the power dynamic, then spend energy trying to win approval from people you've elevated."

**Questions asked:**
1. "The 'kings on thrones' framework from therapy makes sense of a lot... You said you create the throne. What does it feel like to put someone on that throne? And what do you get from doing it?"
2. "You identified your core belief: 'I am not enough, I am a fraud, I am dumb.' **When do you first remember feeling that way? And who in your life has made you feel it was true?**"

**Approach:**
- Explicitly connects pattern to validation-seeking
- States "it's systemic" as interpretation
- Questions probe origins (when did it start, who caused it)
- More analytical tone

#### v0.3 Daily Frontend

**Structure:**
- Two paragraphs maximum
- Flows like compassionate note, no rigid sections
- Questions integrated naturally

**Full text (Paragraph 1):**
> "You're doing some deep work here—connecting the therapy conversation about positioning people as 'kings on thrones' to the feelings you have around Jovana, and then tracing it back through other relationships like with Pazas, Kostas, Alex, and Teo. The way you moved from describing the pattern to asking 'what is my belief that makes me feel I am being criticised?' shows you're getting closer to the root, not just the branches."

**Full text (Paragraph 2):**
> "When you identified the belief as 'I am not enough, I am a fraud, I am dumb,' that took courage to name. I'm wondering what you notice about the relationship between that belief and your affirmations—particularly 'I do not seek validation' and 'I don't need affirmations by others.' What feels true when you hold both those things in your mind at the same time? The work you're doing to understand your own iceberg—behaviors, feelings, thoughts, beliefs—is exactly the kind of self-exploration that creates real change."

**Questions asked:**
1. "What you notice about the relationship between that belief and your affirmations?"
2. "What feels true when you hold both those things in your mind at the same time?"

**Approach:**
- Acknowledges the work user is doing ("you're doing some deep work")
- Highlights the courage it took to name the belief
- Does NOT explain what the pattern means or where it comes from
- Questions invite user's own sense-making
- No investigation of origins or causes
- Tentative language: "I'm wondering what you notice..."

**Key Difference:**
- v0.1 asks "When do you first remember feeling that way? And who in your life has made you feel it was true?" (investigative, potentially imposing trauma narrative)
- v0.3 asks "What feels true when you hold both those things in your mind at the same time?" (evocative, invites present-moment awareness)

### Example 2: February 3, 2026 (Trust Pattern Discovery Day)

#### v0.1 Daily Frontend

**Key Themes excerpt:**
> "You did the trust exercise from The Connection Playbook and discovered a significant pattern: you invest more energy in relationships where trust is lower... You're working harder to win someone who doesn't treat you well."

**Connections excerpt:**
> "This ties together the entire week... Today: the mechanism is clear. You invest more energy where trust is lower, trying to prove yourself worthy and 'win' people who haven't earned your trust. This explains Jovana, the DJ set anxiety (strangers' judgment), and even chasing stakeholder recognition at work instead of deepening craft."

**Questions asked:**
1. "You realized you spend more energy trying to make sure the relationship won't break with Jovana (who you trust less) while being complacent with Ilias (who you trust). You're working harder to win someone who's selfish, passive aggressive, and sneaky. **Why do you think you invest more where trust is lower?**"
2. "You connected yesterday's realization... to Teodora saying 'struggle is pointless' and undermining inner work... **What does it tell you that this insight came only after leaving that relationship?**"

**Approach:**
- States "the mechanism is clear" (certainty language)
- Connects pattern across all contexts (Jovana, DJ set, work) as single explanation
- Characterizes Jovana using user's language but presents it as fact
- Questions ask "why" (investigative)

#### v0.3 Daily Frontend

**Full text (Paragraph 1):**
> "You're noticing something important: that you feel more distant from self-reflection work than you were last year, and you're connecting it to how Teo's perspective might have undermined the value of this kind of inner work for you. The awareness that 'months passed without meditating, without writing, without reading' is significant—you're recognizing what's been missing and why it matters to you."

**Full text (Paragraph 2):**
> "The pattern you identified with Ilias versus Jovana is really interesting: being more laid back with people you trust (because they're 'sealed cases') versus putting more energy into relationships where trust is lower. You even wondered aloud if the AI would catch this pattern later—you're already tracking your own process with curiosity. What I'm wondering is: what happens when you bring that same curiosity to your relationship with yourself? You wrote about wanting to 'come closer to myself,' and it seems like this writing streak is part of that journey. The fact that this entry 'runs smoother and faster' suggests you're finding your rhythm again."

**Questions asked:**
1. "What happens when you bring that same curiosity to your relationship with yourself?"

**Approach:**
- Describes pattern as "really interesting" (not "the mechanism is clear")
- Uses tentative language: "might have undermined" (not "did undermine")
- Does NOT connect pattern across all contexts in daily reflection
- Celebrates user's metacognitive awareness ("tracking your own process")
- Question redirects curiosity to self-relationship (evocative, forward-looking)
- No "why" questions about pattern origins

**Key Difference:**
- v0.1 states pattern "explains Jovana, the DJ set anxiety, and even chasing stakeholder recognition" (imposes unified interpretation)
- v0.3 notes pattern is "really interesting" and asks user to apply same curiosity to self (supports user's own exploration)

## Hypothesis Disclosure Comparison

### v0.1 Approach: Automatic Disclosure

In daily reflections, v0.1 regularly disclosed interpretive hypotheses:

**Example (Jan 30):**
> "This 'kings on thrones' pattern explains the validation-seeking you named on Jan 28. It's not just about Jovana - it's systemic."

**Example (Feb 3):**
> "the mechanism is clear. You invest more energy where trust is lower, trying to prove yourself worthy and 'win' people who haven't earned your trust."

**Characteristics:**
- Hypotheses presented as likely explanations
- Single interpretation offered
- Certainty language ("explains," "is clear," "is systemic")
- Connections made for user across contexts

### v0.3 Approach: Default to Questions, Hypotheses On-Demand

In daily reflections, v0.3 avoided hypothesis disclosure entirely:

**Example (Jan 30):**
> "I'm wondering what you notice about the relationship between that belief and your affirmations..."

**Example (Feb 3):**
> "The pattern you identified with Ilias versus Jovana is really interesting... What happens when you bring that same curiosity to your relationship with yourself?"

**In weekly reflection, v0.3 offers hypotheses BUT with multiple alternatives:**

**Example (Weekly, Validation Pattern):**
> "One possibility is that these might all connect to the belief you named on Thursday: 'I am not enough, I am a fraud, I am dumb.' When that belief is active, maybe external validation becomes a way to quiet it...
>
> Or it could be that these are actually separate things that just happen to look similar. The anxiety about playing music for the first time might be normal first-time jitters, not necessarily about validation...
>
> Or perhaps it's something in between—the underlying belief creates a sensitivity to external judgment, but it shows up differently in different domains depending on what's at stake.
>
> What's your sense of how these connect, if they do?"

**Characteristics:**
- Daily: No hypothesis disclosure, only evocative questions
- Weekly: 2-3 competing hypotheses with equal weight
- Epistemic hedges throughout ("might," "could," "one possibility")
- Explicitly invites user's interpretation
- Validates user authority ("if they do" allows for them NOT connecting)

## Language Comparison

### Certainty Language

#### v0.1 Uses:
- "This explains..."
- "The mechanism is clear"
- "It's systemic"
- "This ties together the entire week"
- Direct causal statements

#### v0.3 Uses:
- "One possibility is..."
- "This might suggest..."
- "I'm wondering if..."
- "Could it be that..."
- Tentative framing throughout

### Question Types

#### v0.1 Questions:
- Investigative: "When do you first remember feeling that way?"
- Causal: "Why do you think you invest more where trust is lower?"
- Interpretive: "What does it tell you that this insight came only after leaving that relationship?"

#### v0.3 Questions:
- Evocative: "What feels true when you hold both those things in your mind?"
- Present-focused: "What happens when you bring that same curiosity to your relationship with yourself?"
- Forward-looking: "What would it be like to keep asking that question as a regular practice?"

## Research-Based Changes Implemented in v0.3

### 1. Default to Socratic Questions
**Research basis:** Socratic questioning shows session-to-session symptom change in CBT; evocative questions facilitate self-exploration in MI.

**v0.1:** Interpretations first, questions second
**v0.3:** Questions first (daily), interpretations on-demand with hypotheses (weekly)

### 2. Epistemic Hedging
**Research basis:** Psychodynamic research on tentative interpretation delivery preserves client agency.

**v0.1:** "This explains," "the mechanism is clear"
**v0.3:** "This might suggest," "one possibility," "I'm wondering if"

### 3. Multi-Hypothesis Presentation
**Research basis:** ACH research shows presenting competing alternatives reduces confirmation bias.

**v0.1:** Single interpretation
**v0.3:** 2-3 alternatives with equal weight

### 4. User Authority Validation
**Research basis:** Collaborative empiricism in CBT; self-determination theory's autonomy support.

**v0.1:** Questions probe for answers AI expects
**v0.3:** Explicit validation of user's authority ("Does this resonate, or am I off-base?")

### 5. Tentative Language for Pattern Explanation
**Research basis:** Epistemic humility research; interpretations are "tentative approximations, not definitive truths."

**v0.1:** "This ties together the entire week... the mechanism is clear"
**v0.3:** "I'm noticing this theme appeared... What's your sense of how these connect, if they do?"

### 6. Protection of Aspirational Affirmations
**Research basis:** v0.1 already protected affirmations; v0.3 added nuance distinguishing aspirational vs. explanatory beliefs.

**Both versions:** Protected affirmations from challenge
**v0.3 addition:** Explicitly distinguishes aspirational affirmations (always protected) from explanatory beliefs (open to gentle collaborative exploration)

## Impact on User Experience

### What v0.1 Offers:
- **Faster pattern recognition:** AI connects dots explicitly
- **Clear framework:** User sees AI's interpretation of patterns
- **Teaching:** Questions teach psychological concepts through inquiry
- **Efficiency:** Don't have to discover everything yourself

### What v0.1 Risks:
- **Confirmation bias:** User might anchor on AI's interpretation
- **Reduced agency:** AI doing interpretive work for user
- **Premature closure:** Single interpretation may prevent deeper exploration
- **Dependency:** User relies on AI to make meaning

### What v0.3 Offers:
- **User-generated insights:** Patterns discovered by user feel more authentic
- **Preserved agency:** User maintains authority over meaning-making
- **Reduced confirmation bias:** Multiple hypotheses prevent anchoring
- **Collaborative exploration:** AI supports, doesn't impose
- **Metacognitive development:** User learns HOW to reflect, not just WHAT to think

### What v0.3 Risks:
- **Slower process:** User must do more work to connect patterns
- **Potential frustration:** Some users may want direct answers
- **Missed patterns:** User might not see connections AI sees
- **Requires engagement:** Works best with active, curious users

## Example Pattern Recognition Comparison

Day 2: (Connects performance anxiety to validation pattern)
Day 3: "This pattern explains the validation-seeking"

**Result:** Pattern explicitly named and connected across contexts BY THE AI in daily reflections.

#### v0.3 Approach:

**Daily reflections:**

**Weekly reflection:**
Only in weekly does v0.3 offer connection hypothesis:
> "Across the week, something about seeking validation from others showed up repeatedly—with Jovana, in the anxiety before playing music, in your reflections on chasing 'stakeholder recognition' at work. I'm noticing this theme appeared in different contexts...
>
> One possibility is that these might all connect to the belief you named... OR it could be that these are actually separate things... OR perhaps it's something in between...
>
> What's your sense of how these connect, if they do?"

**Result:** Pattern connection offered as HYPOTHESIS in WEEKLY, with ALTERNATIVES, inviting USER'S interpretation.

## When to Use Each Approach

### v0.1 May Be Better For:
- Users who prefer direct feedback and guidance
- Early stages when user needs help recognizing patterns
- Users with less psychological literacy
- Time-constrained reflection (want quick insights)
- Educational contexts (teaching psychological concepts)

### v0.3 May Be Better For:
- Users who value autonomy and self-discovery
- Users with some psychological sophistication
- Therapeutic contexts (avoiding confirmation bias critical)
- Long-term practice (building user's own reflection capacity)
- Users concerned about AI influence on thinking
- Research-informed practice (evidence-based safeguards)

---

## Hybrid Possibilities

A hybrid approach might:

1. **Start with v0.3** (evocative questions, user-generated insights)
2. **Offer v0.1 option** ("Would you like to see some patterns I'm noticing?")
3. **User chooses disclosure level** (preference setting)
4. **Adapt based on user engagement:**
   - High engagement + self-discovery → Stay v0.3
   - User stuck or frustrated → Offer v0.1 style interpretation
5. **Always use v0.3 for sensitive patterns** (core beliefs, trauma, shame)

---

## Key Takeaway

The fundamental difference is **who generates meaning**:

- **v0.1:** AI interprets, user responds
- **v0.3:** User discovers, AI supports

Both have value. The research suggests v0.3 better preserves user agency, reduces confirmation bias, and supports collaborative empiricism—but v0.1 may be more efficient and educational for some users and contexts.

The choice depends on:
- User preferences
- Context and goals
- Stage of therapeutic process
- Concern level about confirmation bias
- Value placed on user autonomy vs. guidance

## Specific Examples of Language Shifts

### Connecting Patterns Across Days

**v0.1:**
> "This 'kings on thrones' pattern explains the validation-seeking you named on Jan 28."

**v0.3:**
> "You're doing some deep work here—connecting the therapy conversation about positioning people as 'kings on thrones' to the feelings you have around Jovana, and then tracing it back through other relationships."

**Difference:** v0.1 makes the connection; v0.3 celebrates USER making the connection.

### Identifying Mechanisms

**v0.1:**
> "the mechanism is clear. You invest more energy where trust is lower, trying to prove yourself worthy and 'win' people who haven't earned your trust."

**v0.3:**
> "The pattern you identified with Ilias versus Jovana is really interesting: being more laid back with people you trust (because they're 'sealed cases') versus putting more energy into relationships where trust is lower."

**Difference:** v0.1 states what the mechanism IS; v0.3 describes what USER identified and notes it's interesting.

### Asking Questions

**v0.1:**
> "When do you first remember feeling that way? And who in your life has made you feel it was true?"

**v0.3:**
> "What feels true when you hold both those things in your mind at the same time?"

**Difference:** v0.1 investigates origins (when, who); v0.3 invites present awareness (what feels true).

### Explaining Connections

**v0.1:**
> "This ties together the entire week. Jan 28: you named validation-seeking. Jan 30: therapy gave you 'kings on thrones.' Today: the mechanism is clear."

**v0.3 (Weekly only):**
> "This week told a story about validation, courage, and self-discovery. You started Monday recognizing a validation-seeking pattern... worked through performance anxiety... named a core belief... by the end of the week were identifying trust patterns."

**Difference:** v0.1 explains HOW things tie together; v0.3 describes the NARRATIVE arc and lets user make connections (or offers hypotheses in weekly with alternatives).
