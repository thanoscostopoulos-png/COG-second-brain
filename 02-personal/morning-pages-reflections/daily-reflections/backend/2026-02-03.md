### Trust Exercise - Relationship Investment Asymmetry Discovery

"It is this type of work that I touched upon yesterday when the exercise made me ponder who is someone I trust someone i trust less, and then prompted me to consider how I treat each one of them."

**His answers**:
- **Trust**: Ilias
- **Trust less**: Jovana

**Why Ilias**: "I trust Ilias because I have seen how he thinks and how he treats his loved ones"

**Why not Jovana**: "I trust Jovana less because she is selfish, passive aggressive when things don't happen her way and quite often sneaky, indirect in her remarks (where I can feel she is not being honest or straight with me, even though she has no problem expressing herself confidently)."

**The key realization - how he treats each**:

"Then, when i considered how I treat each of them, I realised how sometimes I could be complacent with Ilias, whereas with Jovana I could spend a lot more energy trying to make sure the relationship won't break."

**Self-awareness check**: "Now, i should be careful generalising from this to saying this is how i treat all people I trust and not trust, because in this case ilias lives in Athens and is someone I know for more than a decade..."

**But then the pattern**: "but there might be pattern there, whereby people I trust are considered 'sealed cases' in a way: i know they will be there and I don't need to fight for it, prove myself worthy of them being there, hence I am more laid back and often put less energy in the relationship."

**The other side**: "On the other hand, with people I trust less, I feel then need to compensate and present myself in such a way where I can win them, so I can trust them...? I am not sure, but might be a good idea to keep looking for this pattern."

**Commitment**: "In fact i want to keep looking for this pattern. (I wonder if the ai agent will catch this in a later exercise)"

**Critical analysis**: THIS IS IT. This is the pattern that connects everything:
- With people he trusts: complacent, less energy, "sealed cases"
- With people he trusts less: compensate, present himself to "win them," spend more energy, try to make sure relationship won't break

This is the relationship investment asymmetry. He invests MORE in relationships where trust is LOWER. He's trying to win people who haven't earned his trust. This is "kings on thrones" in action - he elevates people (Jovana) who don't treat him well, then works to earn their approval.

**Why include in frontend**: This is the most important discovery of the week. It connects validation-seeking, "kings on thrones," and explains the Jovana dynamic. He's spending energy trying to win someone he doesn't even trust.
