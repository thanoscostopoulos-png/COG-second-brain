## I. Core Pattern: The Validation Economy

### The Pattern Itself
The most persistent thread across all reflections is your relationship with external validation, particularly from people whose approval feels uncertain. This manifests in three primary ways:

**1. Asymmetrical Relationship Investment**
On Feb 3, you articulated a key insight: *"I realised how sometimes I could be complacent with Ilias, whereas with Jovana I could spend a lot more energy trying to make sure the relationship won't break."*

You described this as a "sealed case" phenomenon - people you trust receive less active investment because you assume their presence is secure, while people you trust less receive compensating energy as you try to "win them over."

**Evidence from your writing**: During Week 5, Jovana appeared in 4 out of 7 entries as a source of validation anxiety. When she gave your app idea "a very lukewarm response," you processed this at length, questioning whether she doesn't believe in you or can't express support. Meanwhile, when Bo, Mike, and Dimos showed up to your DJ set, you wrote: *"fucking legends, I am moved by all of them"* - gratitude rather than analysis.

**2. Pre-Performance Validation Anxiety**
The DJ set on Jan 31 became a test case. You wrote 14 consecutive affirmations about not seeking validation, all variations on: *"I don't seek anybody's validation. I don't seek anybody's looks. I don't seek anybody's confirmation."* The sheer repetition revealed the nervous system's disbelief.

After the set on Feb 1, you processed it with remarkable gentleness: *"Obviously my set wasn't great and my transitions overall decent, although a couple of times the beat fell off. Nobody will remember, its not a big deal."* You focused on what you gained rather than whether you impressed anyone.

**3. Limerence and Women**
On Feb 5, this pattern surfaced in a new context. You woke to responses from Julia and Katerina on Instagram and observed yourself in "anticipation" mode. You connected this to Teodora's framing: she called it *"Limerence for women and seeking validation."*

But here you showed resistance: *"I'm not sure however if that is true... I don't want to overfit observations into generalised patterns. I want to be careful and explore multiple hypothesis."*

### The Core Belief Underneath
On Jan 30, after therapy with Thalia, you named the underlying structure: *"The belief must be that I am not enough, I am a fraud, I am dumb. This belief makes me think people say those things about me, which makes me feel ashamed, so then I don't show up, I isolate and hide."*

You also connected it to how you position people as "kings on thrones" you've created, granting them power to criticize you - even *before meeting Pazas*, you were already thinking about "the impression I will make to him, how he might criticise me."
