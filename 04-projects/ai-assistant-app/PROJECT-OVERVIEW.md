# Self-Exploration App

## What is this project?
An AI-assisted application designed to help users understand themselves more deeply through writing and reflection. This is a passion project aimed at demonstrating that technology can serve noble purposes—respecting the human condition and supporting what humans need to flourish and be happy.

**Core Philosophy**: Carefully crafted AI interactions that guide meaningful self-discovery, using evidence-based therapeutic approaches while respecting the nuances of human-AI versus human-therapist dynamics.

**Key Focus**: Morning Pages processing, emotional tone analysis, therapeutic reflection questions, and pattern recognition across time.

## Current Status
**Phase**: Research & Design

Currently exploring:
- Therapeutic approaches to hypothesis-sharing (CBT, psychodynamic, person-centered)
- Confirmation bias mitigation strategies
- Reflection process design (Socratic vs. transparent hypothesis vs. hybrid modes)

### Design Track
- [ ] **Experiment: Reflection Process Design Variations**
  - Design and test multiple reflection modes:
    - **Socratic mode**: Questions only, no hypotheses revealed
    - **Transparent hypothesis mode**: Full reasoning shared upfront
    - **Hybrid mode**: Questions first, reasoning revealed on request OR multiple competing explanations presented
    - **Multi-hypothesis mode**: Present 2-3 alternative interpretations to choose from
  - Create prototypes for each approach
  - Test with actual Morning Pages to evaluate effectiveness
  - **Informed by**: Research report findings on therapeutic best practices
  - **Output location**: `04-projects/ai-assistant-app/planning/reflection-process-designs.md`
